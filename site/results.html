What the checks establish

The complete from-rest history, output clock, native archive, and implemented discrete-force readback are verified. RMS and maximum component deviations compare the computed velocity against the prescribed target on active native cells. They do not measure error against an independently known continuum solution.

The finite manufactured force depends on grid spacing. The finest core spacing is 1/1024, and core convergence remains unproven. This finite run stops before t = 1 and cannot establish singularity formation or the paper’s infinite all-order cancellation construction.
