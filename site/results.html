What the checks establish

The complete from-rest history, output clock, native archive, and implemented discrete-force readback are verified. RMS and maximum component deviations compare the computed velocity against the prescribed target on active native cells. They do not measure error against an independently known continuum solution.

The finite manufactured force depends on grid spacing. The finest core spacing is 1/1024, and core convergence remains unproven. This finite run stops before t = 1 and cannot establish singularity formation or the paper’s infinite all-order cancellation construction.

Reading the movies

The horizontal x–y slice stays at z = 0 and the vertical x–z slice stays at y = 0. Each plane samples the finest containing native cell at 256² display points without spatial smoothing. The isometric panel uses trilinear reconstruction of native magnitudes for visualization, retaining native center spacing in the core.

All 280 saved states appear in order. Ordinary states hold for 0.2 seconds, the initial rest state for one second, and the endpoint for half a second. GIFs and MP4s use the same holds. Saved simulation times become closer together toward the endpoint, so the playback clock is not proportional to simulation time.
